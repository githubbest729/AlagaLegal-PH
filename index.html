Important Child Protection Reminder

Registering a child under your own name as if you gave birth to them ("simulated birth") is illegal and can harm the child's future inheritance, passport, and identity rights. Lawful paths such as Relative Adoption (RA 11642), Legal Guardianship, or Substitute Parental Authority protect both you and the child.

Where Free Government Assistance Is Available

  • Public Attorney's Office (PAO): Free legal consultation, affidavit drafting/notarization, and court representation for qualified indigent caregivers (subject to PAO Indigency & Merit Tests).
  • Local Social Welfare and Development Office (LSWDO / MSWDO / CSWDO): Free social worker intake, Home Study / Child Case Study coordination, and referrals to NACC/RACCO.
  • National Authority for Child Care (NACC / RACCO): Administrative adoption processing under RA 11642 eliminates expensive court litigation fees; confirm any minimal administrative/documentary requirements directly with RACCO.
  • Integrated Bar of the Philippines (IBP) Chapters: Free legal aid clinics for qualified low-income families.

2. Barangay & Police Clearances
(Indigency, Custody Cert, NBI/PNP)		 Barangay Hall, PNP Station, NBI Often free for Barangay Certificate of Indigency; standard clearance fees for NBI/PNP Request a Barangay Certificate of Indigency if applying for PAO or fee exemptions.
3. Social Worker Assessment
(Home Study & Case Study)		 LSWDO & NACC / RACCO Free public social work service Medical/psychological evaluations requested during assessment may incur hospital/clinic fees.
4. Administrative Adoption (RA 11642) NACC / RACCO No court filing fees; personal document & newspaper publication (if required) costs may apply Verify exact publication/document rules with your Regional RACCO.
5. Birth Certificate Correction
(RA 9048 / RA 10172)		 Local Civil Registry Office (LCRO) Statutory LCRO filing fee + local newspaper publication (for change of first name/sex) Indigent petitioners may inquire at LCRO regarding statutory fee exemptions.
6. Judicial Guardianship / Rule 108 Regional Trial Court (Family Court) Free legal representation if PAO-qualified; court docket/publication fees apply unless exempt as indigent litigant Ask PAO about Pauper Litigant docket fee exemption under the Rules of Court.
